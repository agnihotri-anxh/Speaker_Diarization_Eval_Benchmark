Voice Arena Executive Brief

Speaker Diarization Evaluation: 1-Page Summary

Architecture, Core Choices & Strategic Findings for Decision-Makers
Author: Ansh Agnihotri
Format: Executive One-Pager
Time-to-Read: 2 Minutes
The Problem: Production voice systems break on cross-talk, 8 kHz phone lines, and reverberant rooms, while vendor marketing claims "99% accuracy" on clean audio. Voice Arena provides an independent, multi-dimensional leaderboard evaluating accuracy (DER @ 0ms/250ms), latency (RTF), cost ($/hr), and overlap resilience across 10 commercial and open-source models.

1. Target Personas & Core Decision Matrices

B2B Meeting Bots (Zoom/Notion)

Acoustics: 3–8 speakers, high cross-talk, AirPods/laptop mics.

Decision: Minimizing action-item attribution errors during multi-person interruptions under $0.015/min.

Contact Centers (Gong/Genesys)

Acoustics: 2 speakers, 8 kHz telephony, Opus/G.711 codecs.

Decision: Zero speaker-flipping on phone audio; sub-500ms streaming latency for live agent assist.

Clinical Scribes (Abridge/Epic)

Acoustics: 2–4 speakers, far-field tablet mic, reverberant room.

Decision: On-premise HIPAA deployment with zero missed speech on soft, hesitant patient voices.

2. Core Methodological Design Choices

Design Choice Voice Arena Protocol Why It Matters to Product Teams
Collar Tolerance Window Dual Reporting: 0ms (Default) & 250ms Academic 250ms collars forgive half a second of error per turn. Real-time voice agents fail with 250ms jitter. 0ms collar tests true boundary precision.
Overlapping Speech Fully Scored + Isolated Overlap DER Cross-talk represents 10–15% of business meetings. Excluding overlap masks where commercial systems fail most severely.
Speaker Count Prior ($k$) Strict Blind Mode ($k = \text{Unknown}$) In production, speaker counts are never known in advance. Providing oracle speaker counts artificially inflates scores by 30–50%.
Cross-Model Fairness 300ms Inter-Word Pause Bridging Converts word-level ASR diarizers (Deepgram) into continuous RTTM segments for fair acoustic comparison against pyannote.

3. Curated Test Corpus (50 Hours Across 4 Production Domains)

To prevent training data contamination, we deploy a proprietary 50-hour golden set: B2B Meetings (20h, 3–8 spks), Telephony (15h, 2 spks, 8kHz), Clinical Consults (10h, far-field), and Broadcast Debates (5h, rapid turns). Dialect stratified: 40% US, 20% UK, 20% Indian, 20% ESL. Ground truth verified via dual human annotation + Montreal Forced Alignment (IAA Kappa > 0.92).

Key Product Takeaway: There is no universally "best" diarization model. pyannote 3.1 dominates complex multi-speaker meeting overlap (3x lower overlap error than cloud APIs), while Deepgram Nova-3 is the clear champion for 2-speaker telephony and live streaming speed.
