The Big Takeaway

There is no single best model for everything:

• Zoom Meeting Bots: Pick pyannote 3.1. It handles multi-person interruptions 3x better than cloud APIs.

• Call Centers (Phones): Pick Deepgram Nova-3. It hits a tiny 1% error rate on 8 kHz phone calls with blazing 190ms speed.
